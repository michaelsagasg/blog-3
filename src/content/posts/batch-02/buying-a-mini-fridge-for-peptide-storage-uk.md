## A dedicated mini fridge solves a specific problem

A standard kitchen fridge cycles temperature every time the door opens and often runs colder near the back and warmer near the door. A dedicated mini fridge, used only for peptides and kept in a stable spot, holds a steadier 2-8°C range and removes the risk of a vial getting pushed to the back near a freezer plate or forgotten behind other food.

### Who actually needs one

If you're storing one or two vials for a few weeks, a clear spot in an existing fridge, away from the door and any freezer compartment, is usually enough. A dedicated mini fridge becomes more useful if you're storing multiple vials long-term, sharing a fridge with people who open it constantly, or want a simple way to track what's inside without digging through food.

## What to check before buying

Not every mini fridge marketed for drinks or cosmetics holds a stable temperature. Look for:

- **A stated temperature range** that includes 2-8°C, ideally with a built-in thermostat dial or digital display rather than a single fixed setting.
- **Compressor cooling rather than thermoelectric cooling** where possible. Thermoelectric mini fridges are cheaper but struggle to hold a stable low temperature in a warm room and are more affected by ambient heat.
- **A separate thermometer**, since built-in displays aren't always accurate. Cheap fridge thermometers are widely available and worth adding regardless of what the fridge itself claims.

### Size and placement

A small 4-10 litre unit is usually plenty for personal peptide storage and fits on a worktop or in a cupboard. Keep it away from direct sunlight, radiators, and anywhere ambient temperature regularly exceeds 25°C in summer, since a small compressor has to work harder in a hot room and may struggle to maintain 2-8°C consistently.

## Where to buy one in the UK

General electronics and homeware retailers sell compressor mini fridges intended for drinks or skincare that work well for this purpose, typically in the £40-£120 range depending on size and brand. A cosmetics fridge marketed specifically for skincare products is often compressor-based and a reasonable choice, since the temperature requirements overlap closely with peptide storage.

### What to avoid

Avoid fridges that only advertise a "cooling below room temperature" claim without a specific range, since these are often thermoelectric units that can struggle to reach or hold 2-8°C, particularly in summer. Also avoid placing any mini fridge, including a good one, somewhere it will be knocked or unplugged accidentally, such as a shared kitchen counter.

## Setting it up correctly

Once you have the fridge, run it empty for a few hours before storing anything, then check the internal temperature with a separate thermometer over a day or two before relying on it. Adjust the thermostat as needed rather than assuming the factory default setting is correct for your room.

### A simple routine that helps

Keep the fridge in a fixed spot, check the thermometer reading every few days, and avoid opening it more than necessary. A dedicated fridge used only for peptides doesn't get opened dozens of times a day the way a kitchen fridge does, which is one of the main reasons it holds temperature more reliably.

## What this doesn't replace

A mini fridge helps with storage once your peptides have arrived; it doesn't address how they were kept cold in transit before that. Combining a reliable storage fridge at home with a supplier who ships in genuinely insulated packaging, backed by a stated dispatch cutoff and batch documentation, covers both ends of the chain rather than just one.

### Running it in before you rely on it

Before storing anything valuable in a newly bought mini fridge, run it for 24-48 hours empty and check the internal temperature with a separate thermometer at a few different points during that window. This catches a unit that's faulty out of the box, or one whose thermostat dial doesn't correspond to the temperature you'd expect, before you've committed a vial to it.

## Bottom line

A dedicated compressor mini fridge with a stated 2-8°C range, checked against a separate thermometer and kept in a stable spot away from heat sources, is a straightforward and relatively inexpensive way to remove storage-related guesswork from the equation.
