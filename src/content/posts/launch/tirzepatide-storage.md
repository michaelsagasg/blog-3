## Unopened vials

Manufacturer guidance for tirzepatide (as used in approved products like Mounjaro and Zepbound) specifies refrigerated storage at 2-8°C before first use. Some approved pen formulations also allow a single, time-limited period at room temperature, typically up to a few weeks, though this varies by product and shouldn't be assumed without checking the specific item's own documentation.

### Why the approved-product guidance is a useful reference

Because tirzepatide is an approved pharmaceutical in several markets, its manufacturer storage data is some of the more thoroughly documented in this space, and it's a reasonable general reference point even for vials sourced differently.

## After reconstitution or first use

Once a vial has been opened, reconstituted, or a pen has been used for the first time, the usable window shortens. Refrigerated storage remains the standard recommendation, generally for a period of weeks rather than months, alongside protection from light.

### Room temperature tolerance

Brief periods at room temperature, such as during a short delivery window, are generally considered less damaging than repeated temperature swings or extended heat exposure. Consistency matters more than any single short excursion.

## A simple storage routine

Refrigerate on arrival, keep the vial in its original packaging or a dark container, avoid the fridge door, and note the date it was first used or reconstituted so the shelf-life clock is easy to track.
