## The standard range

Refrigeration between 2-8°C is the commonly cited storage range for lyophilised retatrutide powder, matching the guidance given for most peptides in this category. This range balances slowing degradation against the risk of freeze damage.

### Why not just freeze it for maximum stability

It might seem logical that colder is always better, but freezing introduces its own risk: ice crystal formation during freezing and thawing can physically damage a peptide's structure. That's why the standard guidance is refrigeration, not freezing, for the powder form.

## What happens outside that range

Above 8°C, the rate of chemical degradation increases, though a brief excursion (during shipping, for example) is generally considered lower risk for powder than for a reconstituted solution. Sustained heat exposure, well above room temperature, is treated more seriously.

### Room temperature shipping

Many suppliers ship lyophilised peptides with cooling measures even though the powder form is comparatively more stable, simply because shipping conditions (a hot delivery van, a parcel left on a doorstep) are unpredictable and outside anyone's control once the parcel leaves the depot.

## After arrival

Moving a vial into proper refrigerated storage as soon as it arrives, rather than leaving it on a counter until convenient, is the simplest way to minimise any accumulated temperature exposure from transit.
