## Faster dispatch means a shorter cold-chain window

Every hour a parcel spends in transit is an hour an ice pack has to hold its temperature. A same-day dispatch policy, with orders placed before a stated cutoff going out that day, directly shortens the total time a peptide spends outside controlled refrigeration.

### Why the cutoff time is worth checking

Missing a same-day cutoff by a few minutes can mean the order sits until the next working day before it's even collected by a courier, adding a full day to the total transit time and to how long the ice pack needs to last.

## What same-day dispatch doesn't guarantee

Dispatching same-day doesn't automatically mean the parcel is properly insulated. The two are related but separate: a supplier could dispatch quickly with poor packaging, or dispatch a day late with excellent packaging. Both matter, and it's worth confirming both rather than assuming a fast dispatch time means everything else was handled well too.

### Weekend and holiday gaps

A same-day dispatch cutoff on a Friday afternoon often means the parcel doesn't move again until Monday, extending transit time significantly over a weekend. Ordering with that timing in mind avoids an unnecessarily long, uncontrolled gap.

## The practical takeaway

Same-day dispatch is a genuinely useful signal, but it's one part of a cold-chain picture, not the whole thing. Pairing it with proper insulated packaging is what actually protects the product.
